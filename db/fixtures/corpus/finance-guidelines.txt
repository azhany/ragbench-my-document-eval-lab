RM-01 Federal Cost Management Guidelines
========================================

Section 1: Approval workflow.
Every ministry expenditure request must receive (1) technical review by the
division cost officer, (2) certification by the head of finance, and (3)
counter-signature by the controlling officer before commitment. Requests above
RM 500,000 additionally require the ministry tender board to convene within
five working days of technical review.

Section 2: Annual budget submission.
Ministries submit their annual budget estimates two months before fiscal year
start. Late submissions are returned once for correction and thereafter
escalated to the controlling officer.

Section 3: Procurement thresholds.
Direct purchase is permitted up to RM 5,000. Quotations (minimum three) are
required between RM 5,000 and RM 100,000. Above RM 100,000 open tender applies.

Section 4: Emergency procurement.
Emergency procurement may bypass open tender with written justification, but
must be reported to the audit committee within 14 days of commitment.

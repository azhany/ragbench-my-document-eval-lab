HR Practice Handbook — RAGbench-MY demonstration corpus (fictional)
===================================================================

Section A: Annual leave.
Staff are entitled to 21 paid leave days per year. Unused leave may be
carried forward up to 5 days into the next year; carried-forward days are
credited to work only and never paid out in cash.

Section B: Leave application.
Applications are lodged in the leave portal at least 12 working days before
the first day of leave. Leave longer than 10 consecutive days requires
counter-approval by the department head.

Section C: Probation.
Probation lasts six months. Performance is reviewed twice, at month two and
month four. Up to 10 paid sick leave days apply during probation.

RM-02 Data Sharing Policy (Internal, fictional demonstration corpus)
====================================================================

1. Scope. This policy governs sharing of non-personal operational data
between departments of the demonstration agency.

2. Data sharing agreement. Every cross-department share requires a signed
data sharing agreement naming the dataset owner, permitted purposes, and a
review date no later than 24 months after signature.

3. Personal data. Any dataset containing personal data must first pass an
anonymisation review; de-identified logs retain latencies and totals but drop
identifiers, free-text remarks, and device fingerprints.

4. Retention. Shared extracts expire 90 days after the review date unless the
agreement is renewed. Expired extracts are deleted and the deletion recorded
in the register.

5. Reporting. The register of active agreements is reviewed quarterly.
